# Troubleshooting

## There is no sound

- In **Manual** mode you have to turn the handle. Choose `Auto` to play on its own.
- Check that the volume in the gear window is not 0. Volume only affects your own PC.
- While recording from YouTube, the music box plays about 17 seconds after the original. Wait while the paper travels to the comb.

## I can't see the control screen

It appears when you are within about 2.2 m of the music box, and disappears when you are too far away.

## YouTube recording doesn't work

- Recording is **PC only**. Quest shows a notice instead.
- Age-restricted, private or region-locked videos may not open. Try another video.
- While someone else is recording, everyone sees the recording screen. Start after that recording ends.
- If you already have 10 saved songs, you cannot start recording. Delete an old song with `×` first.

## The recording sounds very different from the original

Notes are detected live inside the world, so it is not perfect.
Videos played on a single instrument, such as piano or music box covers, work best. Songs with loud vocals or a lot of accompaniment may come out simpler or with wrong notes.

## My saved songs disappeared

- The list only shows songs saved by **people currently in the instance**. A song someone else saved leaves the list when they leave. Tap their name to save songs you want to keep.
- Saves are per world. Songs saved on a music box in another world do not appear.
- Right after you join, your saved data is still loading, so saving may not work for a moment. Wait a little and try again.

## Song names show as boxes

Song names and player names use each PC's OS fonts. Most languages show up fine, but some characters may not appear if the OS has no font for that language.

## The metal parts are too dark

Without a Reflection Probe, metal can look black. Place a Reflection Probe near the music box and bake it.
