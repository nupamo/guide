# Spinning Wheel Music Box

<div class="video"><iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/IXuZD89Atw8" title="Spinning Wheel Music Box Demo" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" allowfullscreen loading="lazy"></iframe></div>

Spinning Wheel Music Box is a hand-cranked paper-score music box you can place in a VRChat world.

The paper score loops around a wheel shaped like a spinning wheel. Each time a punched hole reaches the comb, a note plays.
Turn the handle faster and the song speeds up. Let go and it slows to a stop, like a real music box.

## What it can do

- Play by turning the handle yourself (Manual), or let it repeat on its own (Auto)
- Paste an MML score and play it right away (up to 6 tracks, 6,000 characters; Mabinogi MML works too)
- Paste a YouTube link and punch a new score while the song plays (PC only)
- Save the songs you made to the world data so they are still there next time (10 songs per person)
- Hear the songs everyone in the instance has saved, all in one list
- Switch between 4 instruments: Music box, Marimba, Piano, Clavichord

Everyone in the instance hears the same song at the same time.
