# How to Play

Walk up to the music box and a control screen appears above it. Walk away and it disappears.
Each person sees their own screen, and the text follows your VRChat language (English, Japanese, Korean, Chinese).

## Song list

| Mark | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Tap a row | Plays that song. Tap the playing row again to stop. |
| `×` | A song you saved. Tap it to be asked whether to delete it from your list. |
| Player name | A song someone else saved. Tap it to be asked whether to save it to your list too. |
| No mark | A built-in song that comes with the world. |

New songs appear at the top, newest first. Built-in songs are below them.

## Turning the handle

Pick how it plays with `Manual` / `Auto` under the list.

- **Manual**: Grab the handle and turn it. The paper moves at your speed and the song plays. Either direction works. No matter how fast you turn, it only goes up to 1.5× the normal speed.
- **Auto**: The song repeats without turning. Grab the handle during Auto and it switches to Manual.

The speed of whoever is turning is sent to everyone, so you hear the same thing when someone else turns it.

## Adding a score

Tap `+ Add score` under the list.

### Paste MML

1. Choose the `MML` tab.
2. Paste a Mabinogi MML score. Multiple tracks can stay separated by `,` as usual. (Up to 6 tracks, 6,000 characters)
3. If you like, type a name in the `Song name (optional)` field at the bottom. You can leave it empty.
4. Press `Add & play`. The song goes to the top of the list and starts playing.

### Make one from a YouTube link (PC only)

1. Choose the `YouTube link` tab and paste a link.
2. Press `● Record`. The box listens to the song and punches holes into the paper as it goes.
3. When the video ends or you press `■ Stop & save`, everything punched so far becomes a score and is added to the list.

- The music box sound comes about 17 seconds after the original. That is the time the punched paper takes to reach the comb.
- Only the person who pasted the link hears the original, very quietly. Everyone else hears only the music box.
- Piano or music box covers work best. Songs with loud vocals do poorly, and songs with a lot of accompaniment turn into a rough arrangement.
- Recording is not available on Quest. Quest players can still see and hear someone else recording.

## Saving

- Songs you make are saved to your VRChat data. They are still in the list when you come back to the same world.
- You can save up to **10 songs**. When you are full, you will be asked to delete an old song with `×` before adding a new one.
- The list combines the songs saved by everyone currently in the instance. The same song shows up as one row.
- When someone leaves, songs only they had leave the list too. Tap their name to save songs you like to your own list.
- `×` only removes the song from your own save. If someone else saved it too, it stays in the list under their name.

## Instrument, language, volume

Tap the gear at the top right of the screen.

- **Sound**: Music box, Marimba, Piano or Clavichord. It changes for everyone right away, even while a song is playing.
- **Language** and **Volume** only affect your own screen and sound.
